Projects

Closed-loop grasping on an industrial arm

Sep 2026 · lab

Wrote the hand-eye calibration for a DUCO GCR5 arm with a fixed RealSense D435i, using a touch-probe method, then ran the lab's SAM3 + AnyGrasp pipeline end to end on the real arm.

  • 1.8 mm calibration residual (8 points), 7.9 mm between two independent sessions
  • Three successful closed-loop grasps over two days
  • Fixed a wrist-flip failure by replacing linear moves with joint moves from URDF IK

GELLO leader arm for the GCR5

Aug–Sep 2026 · lab

Adapted the GELLO teleoperation arm to a 22 kg industrial arm. Matched GCR5 and UR5 joint axes from their URDFs so the UR5 kit could be reused, then did assembly, servo bring-up and wiring. Teleoperation of the GCR5 now runs.

  • 7 Dynamixel XL330 servos on one bus, ~52 Hz read with zero dropped packets

Bimanual data and π0.5 post-training

Summer 2026 · lab

Collected bimanual teleoperation demos, converted them to LeRobot format, and set up π0.5 post-training with openpi. Found the wrist camera was overexposed in most frames, measured it, and re-collected the dataset with the problem fixed.

  • Wrist-camera overexposure 71–85% → 0% after re-collection

Cat-play robot

in progress · personal

Open-source cat toys are open loop: none of them watch the cat. The hard part is a reward signal, detecting from video when a cat is really engaged. Plan: an overhead camera, a few discrete play primitives, behaviour cloning from my own demos, then on-robot learning. Five cats give a built-in test for unseen individuals.
